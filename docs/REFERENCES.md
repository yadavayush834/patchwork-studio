# Reference study (12 November 2026)

What we learned from the user's reference videos and pins, and how each idea becomes something of our own. We borrow how a design works, never its content, characters, brands or layout one-to-one.

## The patterns

| # | Reference | How it works | What we take | What we change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Budarina gift card (video) | Huge condensed serif type; real cut-out flowers stand in front of and behind the letters; light/dark toggle. A gift card tilts in 3D, takes a name, then "send" makes a link; the receiver scrolls to open it. | Type and objects interleaved in depth; a personal object you customise, send and open. | Our own subject and type; a different object (not a gift card for a florist). |
| 2 | TwelveLabs racetrack (video) | Dark wireframe world; a creature made of particles runs along a track of thumbnails; the camera follows; a distance counter; press space to jump; particles burst toward the camera. | A particle creature moving through a wireframe world; scroll or play to travel; a counter. | Different creature and world; our own game. |
| 3 | macbeth "flight" (X / video) | A dragon rendered as a point cloud, coloured by depth (blue/green/violet), wings flapping. | Point-cloud rendering of a painted subject from its depth map (our Diorama pairs make this possible). | Our own creatures from our own paintings. |
| 4 | Hectoroz knight (X / video) | Painted image posterised to a few colours, heavy grain, stop-motion jitter between poses. | Stop-motion "boil" and posterised grain as a filter. | Our own art; applied as an effect anyone can use. |
| 5 | anzomez "gm" (X / video) | Photo collage: a figure floating in a radial burst of glitch lines, a burning house, film scratches. | Radial speed-line burst, collage, film damage. | Our own collage pieces. |
| 6 | Swarm Mentality, Fennel×Diesel, Velvet Cage, You are made of stars (pins) | Editorial posters as heroes: giant condensed type, illustration that overlaps it, hand marks (scribbles, arrows), small UI meta (issue, data readouts). | Poster-grade hero sections with layered type and illustration, marks drawn on screen. | Our own illustrations (generated), stories and type. |
| 7 | Samurai / 1860 / History of Future (pins) | A cut-out figure breaks the frame of a card; a red ink/sun shape behind; numbered slides with arrows. | Figure breaking out of its frame; slide controls. | Our own figures and theme. |
| 8 | ozeum, Bacchus & Ariadne, Renaissance edition, Color Addiction (pins) | Museum/editorial: a painting as the stage, thin-line UI over it, tilted card with the next exhibit peeking in. | A gallery template where art is the interface. | Our own artworks (generated, public-domain-style but original). |
| 9 | Robin portfolio, Jedi dashboard, CyberKongz, game pause menu (pins) | Playful UI systems: notebook paper with stickers and tape; neo-brutalist cards with hard shadows; pixel art breaking out of a window; a brush-stroke game menu. | UI primitives in these languages. | Our own components and content. |
| 10 | Sunset Hills, Slow down, Creative Life, Spectrum, aquarium, study room (pins) | Illustrated scenes with a title card set into them (script + spaced caps); cinematic credits. | Title-card scenes; cinematic type layouts over our painted places. | Our own scenes (Diorama). |

## Build plan, in batches

1. **Type and depth heroes** — patterns 1, 6, 7. *Done:* Airmail (hand-built 3D paper planes looping behind and in front of the word), Murmuration (1,400-bird 3D flock flowing through the word, falcon cursor, the flock can spell the word), Deep (a generated diver cut-out stepping out of a tilting 3D card with a live painted sea). Each has a day/night switch.
2. **Make, send, open** — pattern 1's flow. *Done:* Keepsakes (`src/keepsake/`, `?keepsake=globe`): a three.js snow globe you make (world, sky, snow, engraving), send as a self-contained link, and the receiver unwraps on scroll and shakes.
3. **Particle worlds** — patterns 2 and 3. *Done:* Night Run (`src/particles/runner.ts`: a 5,200-particle procedural fox galloping through a wireframe forest, attract mode, then a jump game with fireflies and a best distance) and Nocturne (`src/particles/owl.ts`: a generated owl painting + depth map rebuilt as ~48,000 points with a shader wing-beat, scatter/gather and three colour modes). Both bundle three.js into their own hero.js (`npm run build:particles`).
4. **Art filters** — patterns 4 and 5: stop-motion boil, posterise-and-grain, radial glitch collage. *Not in the Video Lab* (beta). *Done as hero sections:* Late Set (posterised stop-motion boil in a WebGL shader: five inks, per-frame grain, two painted frames plus a per-drawing wobble field, speed-line note on click), Afterglow (collage: canvas glitch burst, keyed paper cut-outs, film damage at 12 fps, RGB-split glitch on click), Copyshop (photocopier generation loss, a pile of copies, drag-to-smear scanography).
5. **Gallery template** — pattern 8. *Done:* Atrium, a Motion template (`src/motion/atrium*.ts|css`): five new old-master-style paintings; start inside a canvas and step back onto the wall, a 3D sideways walk with a brushwork loupe, a painting with four zoomable details and guide notes, a working ticket desk.
6. **UI kits** — pattern 9. *Done:* a sixth Design Library shelf, "UI kits" (`public/library/<id>/index.html`, no dependencies): Scrapbook (lined paper, flip polaroids, peel-and-stick stickers, red-pen checklist), Hard Edge (brutalist co-op dashboard: press-flat buttons, sparkline stat card, toasts, type-to-confirm dialog), Arcade OS (pixel desktop with draggable windows and a taskbar; Moss the slime levels up and breaks out of its window), Pause (a game pause menu in red ink, keyboard first, with a painted world behind it).
7. **Title-card scenes** — pattern 10. *Done:* a title plane in the Diorama engine (the headline hangs at a real depth inside the painting, so nearer things pass in front of it and it shadows what is behind), `public/scenes/titlecard.js` (opening credit, letterbox, push-in, the title writing itself on; skippable, skipped in previews, reduced motion and repeat visits) and three new Scenes: Hayfield (late summer, the oak in front of the title), Blue Room (an aquarium, the title among the jellyfish) and Night Train (a viaduct under the stars).

Each batch is reviewed in the browser before the next starts.
