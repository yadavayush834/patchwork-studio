Mosslight
The Listening Grove v1.0

A little world
that listens.

Mosslight is an interactive pixel backdrop for the quiet corners of the web. Painted live in canvas, awake to your cursor, and calm enough to sit behind anything you build.

The grove is listening.

+ Move to wake the fireflies.
Click anywhere dark to answer.

— fps

Add the grove.

Drop the script into any page, place the element once, and keep building. The canvas stays behind your content.

<script src="mosslight.js" defer></script>
<mosslight-bg mood="moss"></mosslight-bg>
mood
moss, ember, mono
speed
ambient drift, 0.2 to 2.5
interactive
set to false to make it purely decorative
seed
a number for a different sky and tree line
paused
freeze the scene
inline
fill a section instead of the viewport

Respects reduced-motion settings, pauses when the tab is hidden, and keeps a very small pixel canvas for a light footprint.
